Welcome to the Future of School Management

Our Digital School ID system streamlines administrative tasks, allowing staff to focus more on students and less on paperwork. With quick QR code scanning, students can easily check in for classes, borrow library books, and access school facilities without the hassle of manual sign-ins.

Furthermore, our system enhances security by ensuring that only authorized individuals can access school premises, providing peace of mind to parents and educators alike. By adopting this cutting-edge technology, your school reduces its environmental footprint and sets a benchmark for innovation in education management.

Embrace the future today and transform how your school operates with our comprehensive Digital School ID system.

About Us

We are a team of dedicated IT students passionate about transforming school management through technology. Our Digital School ID project was born out of a need to modernize outdated manual systems and improve efficiency in student and staff identification, attendance tracking, and library management. Inspired by Ethiopia’s digital transformation goals, we aim to make schools smarter and more secure. Our mission is to replace traditional paper-based systems with a fully digitalized solution that benefits students, teachers, and school administrators alike
