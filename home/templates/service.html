Our Services

Welcome to School Digital ID System, a secure and efficient platform for managing student and staff identification. This system allows quick verification, easy access to school facilities, and a seamless library experience for borrowing and returning books. Parents can stay informed with real-time notifications about their child's entry and exit, ensuring better security and oversight. With a reliable and smart digital solution, managing school IDs has never been easier.

Workers attendance

Our digital ID system revolutionizes how worker attendance is tracked and managed. By using unique QR codes assigned to each staff member, the system logs every check-in and check-out in real time, directly into a centralized database. This not only eliminates the need for manual entry and paper logs but also minimizes the risk of time fraud and human error. Administrators can view attendance reports, monitor punctuality, and generate summaries with just a few clicks. With improved accuracy, security, and transparency, managing worker attendance has never been more efficient.

digital book borrowing

Our school digital ID system makes book borrowing faster, smarter, and more organized. Students simply scan their ID to check out or return books, and the system automatically updates the records in real time. This removes the hassle of manual entry, reduces the chances of misplaced or untracked books, and ensures every transaction is securely logged. Librarians can easily monitor borrowing history, due dates, and book availability, which improves overall library management and helps students get the resources they need without delays.

Smart Entry & Exit Alerts 🚀

Our Digital ID System offers a seamless way to track student entry and exit times with just a quick QR scan. Each time a student enters or exits the school, the scan triggers an instant notification to parents, providing real-time updates on their child's location. This feature enhances school security by keeping track of movements and ensuring that parents are always informed about their child's whereabouts. With Smart Entry & Exit Alerts, both students' safety and parental peace of mind are significantly improved. 🚀
