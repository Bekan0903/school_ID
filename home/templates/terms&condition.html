Terms and Conditions

User Responsibilities

  • Students and staff must use their digital ID responsibly.
  • QR Codes are unique and should not be shared or misused.
  • Parents will receive entry/exit notifications and must keep their contact details updated.

Entry & Exit Tracking

  • Scanning the QR Code at designated points registers a user's entry and exit times.
  • The school reserves the right to review these logs for security purposes.

Library Book Borrowing

  • Students can use their Digital ID to borrow books from the school library.
  • Each borrowed book must be returned by the due date.
  • Failure to return a book on time results in temporary suspension of digital ID privileges.

Privacy & Data Security

  • All personal information is securely stored and not shared with third parties without consent.
  • The system collects only necessary data (name, ID, activity logs).

System Misuse & Penalties

  • Any attempt to tamper with the system or use another person's ID is strictly prohibited.
  • Violations may lead to disciplinary actions, including suspension of ID privileges.

Technical Issues & Support

  • If a user faces issues, they should report them to the IT department.
  • The school is not responsible for network failures or unauthorized access due to negligence.

Updates to Terms & Conditions

  • The school may update these terms to improve security and functionality. Users will be notified of any significant changes.

By using the School Digital ID System, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these terms.
